# Safety Policy Authority

Deterministic policy boundary for Ride2View safety decisions. It consumes explicit structured evidence signals, applies versioned rules and persists the decision/audit record. It does not infer guilt, fabricate evidence, call emergency services, or silently suspend accounts.

V1 policies cover verification gates, Care Ride authorization failure, payment anomaly review and route-deviation monitoring. Existing RBAC, payment and dispatch authorities remain authoritative in their own domains.
