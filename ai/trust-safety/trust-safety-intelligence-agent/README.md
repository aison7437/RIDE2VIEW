# Trust & Safety Intelligence

Cross-service, recommendation-only risk intelligence. It accepts structured signals with provenance and produces a deterministic risk assessment. A score is not evidence, a policy decision, an accusation, or an enforcement action.

The agent cannot suspend accounts, approve identity, block payments, assign drivers, contact emergency services, or mutate authoritative state. Unknown signal types are ignored rather than invented. Live telemetry and emergency integrations are not configured in this slice.
